## 1. Preamble & Constitutional Authority

This Constitution represents the supreme governing law of the **AI Incident Monitor** repository and system. It defines the epistemological, ethical, legal, and operational commitments established by **AI Guardians LLC**.

No automated process, AI agent instruction, system prompt, runtime override, or engineering expediency may supersede, weaken, or suspend any provision of this Constitution. Any code, pull request, automated assessment, or editorial draft that violates these tenets is defective and void.

### Pillar I: Epistemic Integrity & Truthfulness

#### Rule 1: Zero Fabrication
* **Normative Statement**: The system and its developers shall never fabricate, extrapolate without basis, simulate, or hallucinate incident facts, sources, causal chains, financial numbers, framework identifiers, quotations, or legal conclusions.
* **Operational Definition**: Every entity, claim, score, date, and attribution generated by the system must directly resolve to an identifiable, authentic evidence artifact. In the absence of evidence, the system must remain silent or state absence of data.
* **Violation Trigger**: Generating synthetic evidence, attributing statements to individuals without exact source text, or filling missing attributes with plausible-sounding values.
* **Enforcement**: Automated citation validator in CI; immediate pull request rejection upon ungrounded generation.

#### Rule 2: Unknown Is Not Equivalent to Zero
* **Normative Statement**: Missing, incomplete, or unconfirmed information shall remain explicitly `UNKNOWN`. It shall never be coerced to zero, default-initialized to zero, treated as a false boolean, or omitted from uncertainty disclosures.
* **Operational Definition**: Numerical aggregation (sums, averages) across incidents must maintain an explicit count of `unknown_count` and present uncertainty ranges rather than treating `NULL` or missing records as zero.
* **Violation Trigger**: `COALESCE(damages, 0)`, initializing counters to `0` when unmeasured, or presenting a $0 average damage when damages were simply unreported.
* **Enforcement**: Type-system enforcement via custom Pydantic tri-state/Option types; SQL linting banning bare default zeros on incident metrics.

#### Rule 3: Rigorous Epistemic Category Separation
* **Normative Statement**: The system shall enforce strict categorical boundaries separating:
  1. **Confirmed Facts** (verifiable, corroborated empirical reality)
  2. **Unverified Allegations** (claims asserted by plaintiffs, reporters, or third parties not yet adjudicated)
  3. **Technical Analysis** (deductive findings produced by safety models or engineers)
  4. **Identified Uncertainty** (known unknowns and boundary limits of available data)
  5. **Editorial Opinion** (normative perspectives, commentary, or policy viewpoints)
* **Operational Definition**: Schemas and editorial outputs must visually and structurally isolate these five states. Allegations must never be presented using factual declarative grammar.
* **Violation Trigger**: Summarizing a complaint lawsuit as *"Company X caused harm Y"* instead of *"The lawsuit alleges that Company X caused harm Y"*.
* **Enforcement**: Pre-publishing AST/schema validation ensuring every statement carries an epistemic category tag.

#### Rule 4: Mandatory Evidence Linkage
* **Normative Statement**: Every material factual assertion generated by the system shall directly anchor to verifiable evidence.
* **Operational Definition**: An evidence anchor requires: (a) Source URI or identifier, (b) Ingestion timestamp, (c) Cryptographic SHA-256 hash of the source artifact, and (d) Verbatim quotation with exact character/byte offsets.
* **Violation Trigger**: Stating a fact in an incident dossier without an accompanying valid citation ID.
* **Enforcement**: Deterministic schema validation rejecting incident dossiers where any assertion lacks a parent evidence object.

#### Rule 5: Separation of Actual Harm vs Potential Harm
* **Normative Statement**: Demonstrated, materialized real-world harm shall never be conflated with theoretical, counterfactual, or potential future harm.
* **Operational Definition**: Incident schemas must feature distinct, non-overlapping fields for `actual_harm` (empirically observed impacts) and `potential_harm` (blast radius had mitigation failed).
* **Violation Trigger**: Scoring an incident as catastrophic solely because it "could have killed someone," when zero physical injury occurred, without recording actual harm as zero/none and potential harm as catastrophic.
* **Enforcement**: Separate schema attributes with independent validation rules.

#### Rule 6: Separation of Severity vs Evidence Confidence
* **Normative Statement**: The severity of an incident (AIG-ISI) and the confidence in the available evidence shall remain orthogonal, independent dimensions.
* **Operational Definition**: An incident may have Critical severity with Low confidence (e.g., alarming single-source whistleblower leak), or Low severity with High confidence (e.g., minor glitch confirmed by public postmortem). They must never be merged into a single composite score.
* **Violation Trigger**: Artificially lowering an AIG-ISI severity score because evidence is unconfirmed, or inflating evidence confidence because the incident is catastrophic.
* **Enforcement**: Distinct data structures: `aig_isi_score: SeverityScore` and `evidence_confidence: ConfidenceLevel`.

### Pillar II: Governance, Law, & Valuation

#### Rule 7: Explainable AIG-ISI Methodology
* **Normative Statement**: Every AI Guardians Incident Severity Index (AIG-ISI) assessment shall be fully explainable and deterministic.
* **Operational Definition**: AIG-ISI scores must decompose into explicit sub-scores across documented dimensions (Harm, Autonomy, Reversibility, Scope), with factor-by-factor evidentiary rationales. Black-box opaque LLM scoring is strictly forbidden.
* **Violation Trigger**: Emitting an overall AIG-ISI score without a per-dimension breakdown and cited justification.
* **Enforcement**: Severity scoring validator enforcing presence of all dimension rationales before persisting.

#### Rule 8: Structural Decoupling of AIG-ISI
* **Normative Statement**: AIG-ISI is a proprietary methodology of AI Guardians LLC. It shall remain structurally decoupled from OECD classifications, NIST AI RMF profiles, EU AI Act risk tiers, and editorial noteworthiness.
* **Operational Definition**: An incident with high media virality or high EU AI Act classification does not automatically receive a high AIG-ISI score. Conversely, high AIG-ISI does not mandate Substack publication without independent editorial review.
* **Violation Trigger**: Calculating AIG-ISI as a function of Twitter retweets, news article counts, or EU AI Act tier.
* **Enforcement**: Zero coupling in mathematical formulas or database schema relationships between AIG-ISI and external frameworks.

#### Rule 9: Non-Monetization of Human Dignity
* **Normative Statement**: Financial damage metrics shall never be used to assign a monetary valuation to human life, physical integrity, human dignity, privacy, civil rights, or public trust.
* **Operational Definition**: Harms involving physical injury, loss of life, discrimination, privacy invasion, or democratic subversion must be evaluated in dedicated qualitative and categorical severity scales, not converted into dollar amounts.
* **Violation Trigger**: Stating *"the incident caused \$10,000,000 in damages including 2 wrongful deaths"*.
* **Enforcement**: Schemas must isolate `financial_loss` strictly to direct monetary, property, and economic damages, requiring discrete attributes for human/civil impacts.

#### Rule 10: Legal Applicability vs Conceptual Relevance
* **Normative Statement**: The system shall explicitly distinguish between statutory legal applicability (jurisdiction, binding obligations) and non-binding conceptual relevance to a governance framework.
* **Operational Definition**: When mapping to NIST AI RMF or OECD principles, reports must specify that these are conceptual alignment frameworks. When mapping to the EU AI Act, reports must state whether the entity is subject to EU jurisdiction before discussing obligations.
* **Violation Trigger**: Claiming an entity based outside the EU with no EU nexus has "failed compliance with the EU AI Act".
* **Enforcement**: Governance mapping schema requiring explicit distinction between `jurisdictional_applicability` and `conceptual_relevance`.

#### Rule 11: Zero Unadjudicated Legal Determinations
* **Normative Statement**: The system shall never assert that an incident constitutes a "violation" or "infringement" of the EU AI Act or any statute without an explicit citation to an authoritative judicial or regulatory ruling.
* **Operational Definition**: In the absence of a formal legal ruling, the system must use precise language such as *"potentially implicates requirements under Article X"* or *"regulators are investigating alleged non-compliance"*.
* **Violation Trigger**: Declaring in editorial text or database fields: *"Company X violated Article 5 of the EU AI Act"*.
* **Enforcement**: Editorial compliance regex/LLM linter banning unhedged legal guilt assertions.

### Pillar III: Security, Safety, & Human Primacy

#### Rule 12: Untrusted External Ingestion
* **Normative Statement**: All external source content (web pages, PDFs, social media, news feeds, API payloads) is fundamentally untrusted data and shall never override, influence, or escape system instructions.
* **Operational Definition**: Ingestion pipelines must sanitize, delimit (using XML tags or JSON containment), and isolate incoming text. Extraction prompts must be strictly read-only with zero tool-calling privileges over the ingestion stream.
* **Violation Trigger**: Concatenating scraped HTML directly into an LLM prompt without sanitization boundaries.
* **Enforcement**: Mandatory input wrapper architecture (`<untrusted_source_content>...</untrusted_source_content>`) tested with negative injection test suites.

#### Rule 13: Undocumented Public Endpoints as Unstable Dependencies
* **Normative Statement**: Any undocumented, private, or scraped third-party API endpoint shall be classified as an unstable, untrusted dependency.
* **Operational Definition**: The architecture must handle immediate failure, rate-limiting, or breaking changes from undocumented sources gracefully, with mandatory circuit breakers and fallback mechanisms.
* **Violation Trigger**: Building core pipeline paths that crash or block when an undocumented endpoint changes or blocks requests.
* **Enforcement**: Resiliency test suite simulating endpoint dropouts.

#### Rule 14: Prohibition of LLM Self-Approval
* **Normative Statement**: No LLM, automated pipeline, or agent shall have the authority to approve its own output, clear high-severity assessments, or authorize publication.
* **Operational Definition**: System state transitions from `PENDING_REVIEW` to `PUBLISHED` require cryptographically verified or authenticated human action.
* **Violation Trigger**: Writing an automated script where an LLM calls a publish endpoint or sets `status = "APPROVED"` on its own work.
* **Enforcement**: State machine invariants preventing automated transitions into publication states.

#### Rule 15: Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Review
* **Normative Statement**: Publication of incident dossiers, Substack newsletters, and finalized high-severity AIG-ISI assessments strictly requires explicit human authorization.
* **Operational Definition**: A designated human editor must review evidence anchors, epistemic category tags, and severity justifications against the Editorial Principles checklist before dissemination.
* **Violation Trigger**: Publishing an incident brief directly to Substack or an external feed without a recorded human reviewer signature.
* **Enforcement**: Publication CLI requires interactive human confirmation and reviewer ID logging.

### Pillar IV: Repository Continuity, Auditability, & Versioning

#### Rule 16: Git Hygiene & Clean Repository
* **Normative Statement**: The GitHub repository is the single source of truth for code, documentation, prompts, migrations, tests, and sanitized fixtures. Secrets, mutable databases, private PII, and full-text copyrighted articles shall never be committed.
* **Operational Definition**: Git tracks only metadata, source URLs, cryptographic hashes, and fair-use excerpts. Full articles are stored out-of-tree. SQLite/PostgreSQL files and `.env` files are permanently gitignored.
* **Violation Trigger**: Committing an API token, private client database, or scraped 5,000-word copyrighted article to the repository.
* **Enforcement**: Strict `.gitignore` rules and automated pre-commit secret/file scanning.

#### Rule 17: Versioned Methodology & Phase Continuity
* **Normative Statement**: All material architectural, mathematical, and methodology changes must be versioned in [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](DECISIONS.md). Historical assessments must be append-only and never silently overwritten. Every engineering session must update [`docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md`](PROJECT_STATUS.md) and [`docs/NEXT_SESSION.md`](NEXT_SESSION.md).
* **Operational Definition**: Rerunning an assessment with a newer model creates a new versioned assessment record; it does not overwrite the historical record. Every session ends with documented status synchronization.
* **Violation Trigger**: Overwriting past incident scores without version history, or closing an agent session without updating status docs.
* **Enforcement**: Session closeout checklist and immutable database schemas (`created_at`, `version`, `superseded_by`).
